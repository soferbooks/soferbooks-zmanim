# Soferbooks Zmanim (website)

`Soferbooks Zmanim.html` is the whole website in one file, built on the soferbooks.com zmanim engine in this
repository. Download it and open it in any browser; every time is computed on your own computer or phone.

- **Today**: one day's zmanim, each with every opinion, and the day's special times (Erev Pesach, the 10th hour on a
  chag, Birkat HaL'vanah from 3 and / or 7 days).
- **Month**: a printable month in the Kollel Zmanim layout, the chosen opinions as columns, with the molad, Birkat
  HaL'vanah and the special days underneath.
- **Places**: Great Neck is built in with its skyline (visible sunrise with no download). Any other place can be added
  by address (OpenStreetMap's Nominatim), by the device's location, or by coordinates, when online; its weather
  averages come from Open-Meteo (ERA5, CC BY 4.0), and its visible sunrise can be downloaded once.
